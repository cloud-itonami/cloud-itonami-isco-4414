# Business Model: Independent Scribing & Document Preparation Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-4414`
- ISCO-08: `4414`
- Occupation: Scribes and Related Workers
- Social impact: access-to-justice, literacy, local-jobs

## Customer

- individuals needing form/letter preparation
- small businesses
- immigrants and non-native-language speakers

## Offer

- form and letter preparation
- document translation support
- certified-copy production

## Revenue

- per-document preparation fee
- certified-copy fee
- subscription for repeat filers

## Trust Controls

- no legal or official document certification without governor gate
- client must confirm accuracy before any document is finalized
- document preparation records are auditable, not editable
